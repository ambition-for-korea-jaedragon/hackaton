Type

Unbounded, a wide rounded display face, carries headlines, brand and step names at heavy weights with tight tracking. Figtree handles all reading text. Buttons are sentence case and start with a verb.

The UI wears the look

Each screen's background is a flat field pulled from the current look: apricot for upload, blush for the breakdown, deep tone while your selfie develops, lip red for sharing. Text on every field is Berry Ink or white, chosen for AA contrast.

The split is the moment

On My Tone the screen divides full bleed: the idol's blush and lip on the left, the translated shades on the right. Swatch blocks underneath stay on their own color fields, so the shift is judged on color, not on white.
